Tips for Maintaining Healthy Bones and Joints

Along with regular consultations at an Orthopaedic Hospital In Mira Road, adopting healthy lifestyle habits can help protect your bones and joints while reducing the risk of future injuries.

Some important recommendations include:

  • Maintain a healthy body weight to reduce stress on your joints.
  • Follow a balanced diet rich in calcium, vitamin D, and protein.
  • Exercise regularly to strengthen muscles and improve flexibility.
  • Practise correct posture while sitting, standing, and lifting heavy objects.
  • Wear appropriate footwear to support your joints and maintain balance.
  • Avoid smoking and limit alcohol consumption, as both can affect bone health.
  • Perform stretching exercises before physical activity or sports.
  • Seek immediate medical attention for persistent joint pain, fractures, swelling, or restricted movement.

The Orthopaedic Specialist In Mira Road also provides guidance on injury prevention, workplace ergonomics, sports safety, and lifestyle modifications to help patients maintain healthy bones and joints throughout their lives.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

When should I visit an Orthopaedic Hospital In Mira Road?

You should consult an orthopaedic specialist if you experience persistent joint pain, back pain, neck pain, fractures, sports injuries, difficulty walking, limited joint movement, swelling, or recurring muscle and ligament injuries.

What conditions does an Orthopaedic Specialist In Mira Road treat?

An orthopaedic specialist diagnoses and treats fractures, arthritis, sports injuries, ligament tears, tendon disorders, spinal conditions, joint pain, osteoporosis, musculoskeletal injuries, and other bone and joint disorders.

Is surgery always necessary for orthopaedic problems?

No. Many orthopaedic conditions can be managed successfully with medication, physiotherapy, lifestyle modifications, injections, and rehabilitation. Surgery is recommended only when conservative treatment does not provide adequate relief.

How long does recovery take after joint replacement surgery?

Recovery varies depending on the patient’s age, overall health, and the type of surgery performed. Most patients gradually resume their normal daily activities within a few weeks, while complete recovery may take several months with proper rehabilitation.

How can I keep my bones and joints healthy?

A nutritious diet, regular exercise, maintaining a healthy weight, practising good posture, preventing injuries, and attending regular medical check-ups all contribute to strong bones and healthy joints.
